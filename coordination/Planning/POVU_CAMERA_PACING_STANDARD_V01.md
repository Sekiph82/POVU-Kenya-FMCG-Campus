# POVU CAMERA PACING STANDARD V01

## Principle

**SLOW ENOUGH TO INSPECT.** The Complete Tour is not an accelerated version of R02. R02 remains the visual-system baseline, but M07 camera timing must give the viewer time to identify architecture, machinery, process relationships and circulation.

## Starting speed ranges

| Movement | Starting range | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Campus aerial establish | 2–6 m/s | Broad orientation; decelerate before a destination enters the focal area. |
| Restrained aerial crane | 0.5–2.5 m/s horizontal, 0.2–1.0 m/s vertical | Roof, solar and production context. |
| Walking-height exterior | 0.35–0.9 m/s | Entrances, plazas, landscape and landmarks. |
| Interior walkway | 0.20–0.65 m/s | Glass Deck, warehouses and production aisles. |
| Machinery inspection dolly | 0.10–0.45 m/s | Tank groups, fillers, wipes and EOL equipment. |
| Gentle arc/orbit | 5–18 degrees/s | Landmark, totem, Water Wall and robot context; stop after the relationship is readable. |
| Near-static hero hold | 0–0.08 m/s | HOG, VIP doors, Water Wall detail, MES and final title. |

These are starting ranges, not a license to move continuously. A shot may hold still while the viewer inspects it.

## Readability rules

- Major campus establishing views: 8–15 seconds minimum.
- Major buildings: 10–25 seconds, with an entrance hold rather than a single pass.
- Landmarks: 10–20 seconds; HOG receives a full sculpture hold and world-tracked label.
- Important architectural experiences: 15–30 seconds; Glass Deck interior is longer because it must be entered and explored.
- Major production areas: 30–75 seconds where multiple stages or machine relationships are present.
- Secondary supporting features: 6–15 seconds, but never if that prevents recognition.
- Important frames hold for approximately 2–5 seconds with only subtle motion.

## Acceleration and deceleration

- Use ease-in/ease-out curves with no instantaneous velocity changes.
- Begin a move only after the previous target has been held long enough to identify.
- Decelerate before a label or machine enters its readable region.
- Do not use whip pans, sudden yaw, sudden pitch, constant drone motion, or three-second location hopping.
- If two features are geographically distant, use an oriented map/route card or a motivated cut rather than a teleporting camera.

## Labels and overlays

- Normally show 1–3 world labels simultaneously.
- Labels must point to the correct object, remain readable, and fade before the camera leaves the feature.
- HOG label text is `HANDS OF GROWTH` with secondary `People • Skills • Future` and must follow `LABEL_ANCHOR_HANDS_OF_GROWTH`.
- Equipment labels use the existing R02 visual language and selected M05 anchors; do not label every repeated tank, pallet or panel.
- Overlays explain route, zone, evidence limitation or process direction; they do not replace a missing visual feature.

## Transition behavior

- Prefer spatially motivated transitions: solar canopy to path, path to VIP, Glass Deck glazing to production, tank outlet to filling, packaging conveyor to pallet, pallet to warehouse, and warehouse to dispatch.
- Use fade-to-black only for a genuine chapter boundary or evidence limitation.
- Preserve compass/zone orientation after every distant cut.

## Acceptance gate for M07

A shot passes pacing QA only when the intended feature can be named from the frame, the camera motion does not outrun inspection, labels do not obscure the feature, and the shot satisfies the linked coverage-matrix criteria. A feature appearing in the background is not a pass.
