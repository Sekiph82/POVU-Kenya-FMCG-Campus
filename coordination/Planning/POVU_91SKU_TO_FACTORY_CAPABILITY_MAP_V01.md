## Factory capability interpretation

- Wet-processing tanks represent shared formulation/process capability, not a SKU-specific recipe.
- Bottle, closure, trigger, filling, sachet, powder, wipes, EOL, warehousing and dispatch routes are shown as capability families.
- 91 SKUs may be used as a portfolio/documentary overlay. It must not be used to imply 91 individually modeled products or 91 dedicated lines.
- Exact equipment names from the Presidential Deck, including the PP+FRP hypochlorite tank and dedicated equipment quantities, remain source-document evidence only where the REV004.1 semantic manifest does not contain the named object.
