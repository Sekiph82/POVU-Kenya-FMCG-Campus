## Execution semantics

Process specs numerically, but geometry changes are cumulative within a facility:
- A_CONTEXT owns major layout/envelope and context camera.
- B_FUNCTIONAL refines primary functional assemblies and functional camera.
- C_SEQUENCE_OR_DETAIL refines connected sequence/detail and close evidence.
- The six preserved groups use A/B specs only; restore their prior accepted visibility rather than redesigning by default.

After changing a facility, re-render every view for that facility before moving to the next one.

No spec may be closed from object names/counts. The resulting image must visually satisfy the acceptance test inside that spec.
